Low confidence: the model is unsure. This often happens with sarcasm, mixed sentiment, very short inputs or text that is not Kinyarwanda.
About. AfroXLMR-small (Alabi et al., 2022) fine-tuned on the AfriSenti-SemEval 2023 Kinyarwanda Twitter dataset (Muhammad et al., 2023); test macro-F1 0.68. The model runs entirely in your browser (ONNX + transformers.js); your text is not sent to a server.

Limitations. Trained on ~3.2k tweets only. Negation (ntabwo…), sarcasm and mocking emojis (😂) are frequent sources of error, and the model is over-confident on text that is not Kinyarwanda. Not suitable for high-stakes decisions.
